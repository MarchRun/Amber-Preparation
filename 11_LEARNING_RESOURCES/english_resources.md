# English Resources

**Created / last updated:** 31 October 2026
**Purpose:** Listen, imitate short chunks, then explain your own work.
**Retrieval status:** Recommended links, not newly fetched pages.

- https://dictionary.cambridge.org/ — hear technical-word stress and variants.
- https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/ — short listening/speaking practice.
- https://youglish.com/ — hear a phrase in context; avoid copying irrelevant mannerisms.
- Local `../07_ENGLISH_PREPARATION/` — grounded scripts and repair phrases.

Use one 20-second listening segment, shadow twice, then explain the same idea in your own words. Practise actual project names and ownership boundaries. Do not try to sound native or learn elaborate idioms overnight.
