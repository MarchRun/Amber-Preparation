## Non-negotiable rules

1. **Guess when stuck.** The standard CCAT has no wrong-answer penalty. Do not let a hard reached item consume the time needed for easier later items.
2. **20-second rule** in full mocks; **15-second rule** in sprints.
3. **Assume one pass until navigation is confirmed.** If forward-only, guess and advance. Return to marked items only if instructions explicitly allow it.
4. **No calculator.** Practise the arithmetic the test actually requires.
5. **Review method, not memory.** If you remember an answer, the item no longer measures anything.
6. **Do not over-train.** Balance cognitive practice with business case and English. Employer component weights are UNKNOWN; two full sets plus review are a practical budget, not a guaranteed sufficient dose.

## What the scores mean

Criteria reports about 24 correct on average and fewer than 1% answering all 50. These are provider-test figures, not norms for our homemade sets. A local target such as 30/50 is only a personal drill goal; it does not establish competitiveness, percentile or Amber's cut-off. These sets also share templates and contain text-heavy spatial questions, so they are not equivalent independent forms.
