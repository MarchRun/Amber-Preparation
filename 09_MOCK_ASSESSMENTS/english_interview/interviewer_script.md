# Ready-to-Run English Script

**Created:** 1 November 2026
**Status:** Original practice prompts, not an observed Amber interview.

## Twenty-five-minute sequence

| Minutes | Prompt | Follow-up / observation |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 | Please introduce yourself and connect your experience to this role. | Stop at two minutes; did the answer distinguish internship from projects? |
| 2–7 | Explain SMB Suvanna Dipa to a colleague who has not seen it. | What was personally yours? What would need confirmation before describing the hardware? |
| 7–12 | Explain authentication and authorization in plain English. | Give one allowed action and one denied action in a hypothetical school system. |
| 12–17 | Summarise a solution for duplicate attendance events. | Tell me one assumption, one failure and one test. |
| 17–22 | Describe a real learning or coordination responsibility from your portfolio. | If asked about failure/conflict instead, do not invent an incident. Distinguish role description from STAR. |
| 22–25 | Practise clarification and repair using the exercises below. | Can you restate the intended meaning accurately? |

## Three repair exercises

1. Facilitator says: "For now, I only need a high-level design, not executable code." Candidate restates the deliverable and asks one scope question.
2. Facilitator asks: "What was the throughput?" If the term or actual figure is unknown, clarify the term and say a measured number is unavailable. Do not invent requests per second.
3. Candidate deliberately starts "I built the RFID hardware...", then repairs: "Let me correct that. My documented contribution is the web application and attendance integration, not hardware development."

## Feedback after the clock

Write the exact phrase that lost meaning, not merely "bad grammar". Distinguish a language misunderstanding from incorrect technical knowledge. Re-record the weakest 30–60 seconds after simplifying sentences. Use one idea per sentence, short pauses and a clear ownership statement.

**Ready-to-use clarification:** "Could you clarify whether you want a diagram, a written explanation or working code?" Do not apologise repeatedly; ask for the exact missing information.
