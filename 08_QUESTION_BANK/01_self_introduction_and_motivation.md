| **Contoh jawaban (ID)** | Salah satu jawaban yang mungkin adalah motivasi membangun fitur operasional yang berguna, karena portofolio saya mencakup administrasi sekolah, pemantauan dan deployment. | **Follow-up** | What kind of task drains you, and how do you handle it when it is unavoidable? |
| **Weak answer / red flag** | "I love coding" with no object; or admitting you lose interest in anything repetitive, which is most of a junior's first months. |
| **Scoring anchor** | 1 = generic enthusiasm with no example · 3 = sincere but unrelated to the job's daily reality · 5 = a concrete moment, a maintenance-sized win, and awareness of the tasks that drain you |
