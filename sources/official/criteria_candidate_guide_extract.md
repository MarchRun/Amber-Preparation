## Other provider statements observed, paraphrased

- Domains are verbal, math and logic, and spatial reasoning.
- Fewer than 1% answer all 50 questions; the page says the average test taker gets about 24 right.
- Employers receive raw correct count and percentile against the provider comparison population.
- The guidance recommends reading instructions, guessing and moving on when stuck, and avoiding excessive time on one item.
- Spatial reasoning involves mentally rotating/flipping images, patterns and outliers.
- Score is only one factor in an employer's decision.
