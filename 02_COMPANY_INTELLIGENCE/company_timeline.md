## 2. Narrative arc

### Phase 1 — Consulting foundation (2016–2020)
Amber appears to have been founded by people with experience in larger consulting environments, focused on Oracle applications and practical delivery. Public evidence is thin, but the company's own narrative consistently references a strong consulting background.

### Phase 2 — Service expansion (2021–2024)
The company added custom development, talent services, and broader ERP coverage (Odoo, Dynamics 365). Employee profiles show frontend, full-stack, and technical lead roles, suggesting a growing delivery team.

### Phase 3 — Product and AI positioning (2025–2026)
FOOMA is marketed as a repeatable financial-automation product. The website reframes the company around AI, intelligent automation, and data. Odoo content targets specific verticals (mining, property). Hiring expands to SAP, AWS, .NET, and Flutter.

**Interpretation:** Amber is trying to move up the value chain from staff augmentation and custom projects toward productised, higher-margin enterprise solutions. This is a common consultancy evolution and creates demand for juniors who can learn quickly across domains.
