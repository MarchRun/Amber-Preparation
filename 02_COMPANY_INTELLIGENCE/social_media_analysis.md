## 3. Themes

### 3.1 Productisation
FOOMA is promoted as a repeatable product with clear value: data unification, validation, month-end close, ERP integration. This suggests a shift from pure bespoke projects toward reusable assets.

### 3.2 Multi-platform ERP
Content covers Oracle/NetSuite, Odoo, Microsoft Dynamics, and SAP hiring. The company does not position itself as a single-vendor shop.

### 3.3 Vertical focus
Recent Odoo articles target property management and mining. This is a classic consultancy play: package expertise by industry to shorten sales cycles.

### 3.4 Employer branding
Hiring posters, "#TalentNetwork" content, and career articles show active talent acquisition. The tone is modern, visual, and somewhat informal.

### 3.5 AI positioning
AI is used as a framing device ("AI-powered applications," "intelligent automation," "computer vision") rather than a specific product claim. Expect interviewers to value practical AI awareness, not deep ML expertise.

### 3.6 Quality and testing
The testing article signals that quality is part of the brand narrative. A business-case answer that includes a test strategy will resonate.

## 6. Limitations and ethics

- Only public, search-indexed content was reviewed.
- No private groups, employee-only posts, or direct messages were accessed.
- Post dates may be approximate due to platform display.
- Social media is marketing; it is not evidence of internal culture or delivery quality.
- Do not quote an employee's personal post in an interview.
