# Questions for the Recruiter

**Created / last updated:** 31 October 2026
**Purpose:** Resolve high-impact unknowns politely.

Priority message draft:

> Thank you for the assessment invitation. Could you please confirm whether the Programmer Business Case involves working code or a written/spoken solution, and whether a programming language is prescribed? Could you also confirm which tools or references are allowed? I will follow the instructions provided on the day.

Other questions, only as relevant:

- Is the cognitive test the standard Criteria CCAT, and what platform will be used?
- Are scratch paper, notes, calculators or additional screens permitted?
- Are there further stages in the 09:00–13:30 window?
- What is the connection-failure procedure?
- Which stack and mentoring arrangement would a new programmer begin with?
- What are the current work location, contract term and review process?
- What is the current compensation band and package? The archived ad is not a current offer.

Do not request actual questions, confidential scoring keys or privileged candidate material. A recruiter may legitimately decline to disclose a cut-off or exact case format.
