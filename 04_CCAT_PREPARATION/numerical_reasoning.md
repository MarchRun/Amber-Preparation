## 2. Mental math toolkit

### 2.1 Fraction ↔ percentage

| Fraction | Percentage |
|---|---|
| 1/2 | 50% |
| 1/3 | 33.33% |
| 1/4 | 25% |
| 1/5 | 20% |
| 1/6 | 16.67% |
| 1/7 | 14.29% |
| 1/8 | 12.5% |
| 1/9 | 11.11% |
| 1/10 | 10% |
| 1/11 | 9.09% |
| 1/12 | 8.33% |
| 2/3 | 66.67% |
| 3/4 | 75% |
| 4/5 | 80% |

### 2.2 Percentage shortcuts
- 10% = move decimal one place left.
- 5% = half of 10%.
- 15% = 10% + 5%.
- 20% = 10% × 2.
- 25% = divide by 4.
- 75% = 3 × 25%.
- Increase by 20% = ×1.2.
- Decrease by 20% = ×0.8.
- Successive changes multiply: +20% then −20% = ×1.2 × 0.8 = 0.96 → 4% net decrease.
- Percent change = (new − old) / old × 100.

### 2.3 Ratio shortcuts
- Ratio a:b means total parts = a + b.
- One part = total ÷ (a + b).
- To split 64 in 3:5 → 8 parts → 8 per part → 24 and 40.
- To compare ratios, cross-multiply.

### 2.4 Averages
- Average = sum ÷ count.
- Sum = average × count.
- If one value is missing: missing = total sum − known sum.
- For evenly spaced numbers, average = (first + last) ÷ 2.

### 2.5 Rates and work
- Speed = distance ÷ time.
- Time = distance ÷ speed.
- Combined work: 1/T = 1/A + 1/B.
- If A does a job in 6 days and B in 3 days: 1/6 + 1/3 = 1/2 → 2 days.
- Units must match (minutes vs. hours).

### 2.6 Estimation
Before calculating exactly:
- Round to friendly numbers.
- Check the order of magnitude.
- Eliminate answers that are obviously too large or small.
- Use the answer choices to guide precision: if options are far apart, estimate; if close, calculate.

## 3. Number series patterns

Check in this order:
1. **Difference** — constant? increasing? alternating?
2. **Ratio** — multiply/divide by a constant?
3. **Alternating** — two interleaved sequences?
4. **Squares/cubes** — 1, 4, 9, 16, 25...
5. **Primes** — 2, 3, 5, 7, 11...
6. **Fibonacci-like** — each term is the sum of the previous two.
7. **Digit manipulation** — sum of digits, reverse, concatenate.
8. **Combination** — ×2 then −1, +3 then ×2, etc.

### Examples
- 3, 6, 11, 18, 27 → differences +3, +5, +7, +9 → next +11 = 38.
- 2, 6, 12, 20, 30 → n(n+1) → next 42.
- 5, 10, 9, 18, 17 → ×2, −1, ×2, −1 → next 34.
- 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8 → Fibonacci → next 13.
- 2, 3, 5, 7, 11 → primes → next 13.
- 1, 4, 9, 16, 25 → squares → next 36.

## 5. Worked examples

### N-EX-01
**Question:** What is 18% of 250?
A) 35 B) 40 C) 45 D) 50 E) 55
**Answer:** C) 45.
**Method:** 10% = 25; 8% = 20; total 45.

### N-EX-02
**Question:** A jacket costs 60 after a 25% discount. What was the original price?
A) 72 B) 75 C) 80 D) 85 E) 90
**Answer:** C) 80.
**Method:** 60 = 0.75 × original → original = 60 ÷ 0.75 = 80.
**Trap:** Adding 25% to 60 gives 75, which is wrong.

### N-EX-03
**Question:** Divide 88 in the ratio 4:7.
A) 32 and 56 B) 36 and 52 C) 40 and 48 D) 28 and 60 E) 44 and 44
**Answer:** A) 32 and 56.
**Method:** 11 parts → 8 per part → 4×8=32, 7×8=56.

### N-EX-04
**Question:** The average of six numbers is 15. Five of them sum to 70. What is the sixth?
A) 15 B) 18 C) 20 D) 22 E) 25
**Answer:** C) 20.
**Method:** Total = 6×15 = 90; sixth = 90−70 = 20.

### N-EX-05
**Question:** A car travels 180 km in 2 hours 15 minutes. What is its average speed?
A) 70 km/h B) 75 km/h C) 80 km/h D) 85 km/h E) 90 km/h
**Answer:** C) 80 km/h.
**Method:** 2h15 = 2.25h; 180 ÷ 2.25 = 80.

### N-EX-06
**Question:** A can finish a job in 12 days; B can finish it in 6 days. Working together, how long?
A) 3 days B) 4 days C) 5 days D) 6 days E) 9 days
**Answer:** B) 4 days.
**Method:** 1/12 + 1/6 = 3/12 = 1/4 → 4 days.

### N-EX-07
**Question:** What comes next? 5, 10, 9, 18, 17, ...
A) 26 B) 30 C) 34 D) 36 E) 35
**Answer:** C) 34.
**Method:** ×2, −1, ×2, −1, ×2.

### N-EX-08
**Question:** A price increases by 40%, then decreases by 25%. What is the net change?
A) 5% increase B) 5% decrease C) 10% increase D) 10% decrease E) No change
**Answer:** A) 5% increase.
**Method:** 1.4 × 0.75 = 1.05.

## 8. Common mistakes

- Adding the discount percentage instead of dividing by the remaining percentage.
- Forgetting to convert minutes to hours.
- Mixing up "percent of" and "percent change."
- Using average as a sum.
- Assuming a series is arithmetic when it alternates.
- Calculating exactly when estimation would eliminate four options.
- Spending 60 seconds on one word problem.
