## Trend rules

1. Record raw correct AND substantive-attempt accuracy. Mark blind guesses separately at submission time; guessed correct answers still count in raw score.
2. A raw-score rise may be useful even if accuracy falls. Interpret pace, difficulty, hints and familiarity instead of using an absolute accuracy rule.
3. If one domain stays below 55% across two attempts, drill that domain untimed before the next full mock.
4. If all domains are above 65% but fewer than 40 questions are attempted, drill sprints only. Content study is no longer the bottleneck.
5. Stop taking full mocks 12 hours before the assessment. One light sprint is enough on the morning itself.

|---|---:|---|

| Accuracy on attempted | 68%+ | Avoids "fast but wrong" |
| Verbal | 12/18 | Vocabulary and logic are trainable |
| Numerical | 13/20 | Arithmetic speed is the main constraint |
