# Sources and Evidence Rules

**Created:** 31 October 2026
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026
**Purpose:** Distinguish primary evidence, research notes and recommended reading.

| Label | Meaning in this project |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Explicitly supported by an identified source; an official marketing statement verifies what the company says, not independent delivery quality |
| CORROBORATED | Consistent across more than one relevant source; sources may still share an origin |
| REPORTED | Attributed third-party/self-report/snippet, not independently established |
| INFERRED | Reasoned conclusion from evidence, not directly stated |
| HYPOTHESIS | A possibility prepared for, without enough evidence to prefer it confidently |
| UNKNOWN | Information unavailable or not established |

Confidence is qualitative unless a probability is empirically supported. Earlier percentages such as 80% are subjective estimates, not measured likelihoods. Fictional practice scenarios are not company intelligence and must be labelled separately.
