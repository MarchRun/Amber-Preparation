# Mode D — English Communication

**Created:** 31 October 2026
**Last updated:** 1 November 2026 — canonical English-interview location.
**Purpose:** Observe comprehension and explanation, not accent conformity.

25-minute practice: 2 introduction; 5 project plus two follow-ups; 5 technical distinction; 5 fictional case summary; 5 behavioural prompt; 3 clarification/repair exercises. Do not memorise a paragraph and skip follow-ups.

Record only your practice with consent of anyone participating. Facilitator should ask naturally and allow requests for clarification. Note whether misunderstanding concerns language or the underlying concept. Ask for one rephrasing, one ownership boundary and one unknown-technology answer.

Use `../../07_ENGLISH_PREPARATION/` after the attempt, not as a live script. Score intelligibility, comprehension, structure, interaction and factual honesty. No score is valid before a sample is heard.
