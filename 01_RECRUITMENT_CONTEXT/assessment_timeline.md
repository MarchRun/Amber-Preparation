## 3. Energy management

A 4.5-hour online assessment is an endurance event.

- **Hydration:** small sips, not large gulps.
- **Posture:** feet flat, back supported, screen at eye level.
- **Breaks:** stand and stretch at every offered break.
- **Food:** light protein + complex carbohydrate before; avoid heavy meals.
- **Attention:** if focus dips, reset with one slow breath and a posture change.
- **Voice:** warm up by reading one paragraph aloud before joining.
