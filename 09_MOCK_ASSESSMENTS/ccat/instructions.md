# Mode A — CCAT-Style Practice

**Purpose:** Measure pacing on original, unnormed items.
**Created / last updated:** 31 October 2026

1. Select `../../04_CCAT_PREPARATION/mock_tests/mock_test_1.md` for baseline or `../../04_CCAT_PREPARATION/mock_tests/mock_test_2.md` for a second full set. Sprint: `../../04_CCAT_PREPARATION/mock_tests/mock_test_3.md`.
2. Use 15 minutes for 50 items or 8 minutes for 30. No calculator, external help or answer key. Scratch paper is allowed for these practices only; actual rules govern the real assessment.
3. Treat navigation as forward-only unless deliberately practising a confirmed revisitable format. At the decision limit, guess and move; do not plan a return by default.
4. Mark each answer as solved/eliminated guess/blind guess at the time. All submitted guesses count in raw score, but are not substantive attempts.
5. Stop at time. Score against `../../04_CCAT_PREPARATION/answer_keys.md`. Do not finish unanswered items before recording the timed score.
6. Use `score_report.md` and review three errors. Mock 2 shares many formats with Mock 1; improvement may reflect familiarity, not general CCAT improvement.

These text-heavy spatial items underrepresent graphical pattern matrices. Supplement with free provider samples listed in section 11. No percentile, IQ, employer cut-off or calibrated CCAT equivalent can be inferred.
